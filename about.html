Katana Calderwood

I'm Katana, an experimental artist, designer, and builder with a professional background in environmental, architectural design. My dream has been to help guide the built environment back to the direction of Earth’s natural landscape through use of what I have dubbed “future-primitive” design which merges innovative technologies with traditional, local methodologies and materials. I value biomimicry, sustainability, and regenerative architecture and believe these practices should be the baseline of communities.

From living and working in Asia, Morocco, and Central America to the extreme deserts of Southern California and the Black Rock Desert, I have created a versatile and exciting portfolio ranging from designing and building rural Berber public restrooms with local stone masons, 300,000 square-meter Korean Hyundai training centers, off-grid communal living experiments to other-worldly stages for Coachella. As Burning Man Project’s first Construction & Planning Specialist, I helped manage the construction process and governmental planning of over 25 projects, including my main project: a complex, multi-million dollar project instrumental to the event's operation.

Throughout the years, my foundation in architectural design sparked a critical realization: our built environment is inherently extractive unless it is meticulously rooted in regenerative design. Driven to understand the macro-forces shaping our ecosystems, I transitioned into land-use planning to study systemic land stewardship and preservation.

Today, my work has evolved into a nomadic, multi-disciplinary practice: My background in spatial design and environmental stewardship blends with my original music and multi-media art to explore the intersections of community, alternative living, and creative technology where I treat sound, physical media, and spatial design as interconnected tools.

In 2026, I was a featured artist at a world-renowned art gathering in Bombay Beach, California with the debut of my immersive, multi-purpose installation Algo(g)rave: The Manual for Dying. The accompanying audio content, mixed and mastered by myself, features originals from over 15 artists from all over the world (including three music groups of which I am a member) and aired on the local radio station, KDEZ 91.1 FM, on Monday, March 30, 2026.

Stay tuned for the Algo(g)rave’s online debut and my move to Europe to start an artist residency community.
